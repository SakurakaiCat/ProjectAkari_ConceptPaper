\section{Glossary}
\label{app:glossary}

\begin{description}
  \item[Akari] Project codename; from the Japanese 「あかり」, meaning a light.
  \item[KC] Knowledge component --- an atomic, independently assessable skill.
  \item[LCMS] Lesson content management system; the lesson AST and publishing
    pipeline that carries course content into the runtime.
  \item[ALM] Akari Lesson Markup --- the authoring markup that lowers to the
    lesson AST.
\end{description}
